# One chain

A SaaS promo with no hard cuts. Every scene hands one object to the next (a bar becomes a pill, then
a button, then a circle). Single UI pieces are lifted out of the real product and shown big over a
blurred or tilted page. Moves land on spoken words and on the beat, and something is always moving.

- **Full recipe:** [docs/film-playbook.md](../../docs/film-playbook.md) (chain table, captures,
  components, timing, music, checks).
- **Example:** `studio/src/films/DittoV3.tsx`.
- **Check:** `check-video.sh` should report 0 hard cuts.
