# Brief

Fill this in before any code. Ask the user only for what you cannot find or safely assume, in one
batched question, with your suggested answer next to each gap. Most rework comes from a missing line
here, not from bad code.

## Audience and message
- **Who watches it:** (new users / existing users / investors / hiring managers)
- **The one message:** (the single sentence a viewer should remember)
- **What they should do after:** (sign up, trade, reply, nothing)

## Where it plays
- **Platform:** (X, LinkedIn, landing page, pitch deck)
- **Length:** (seconds) · **Shape:** 16:9 1920x1080 / 9:16 / 1:1 · **Sound:** on / off (then subtitles carry the story)

## Content
- **Product access:** (URL, test login, scratch database; what is safe to touch; what is read-only)
- **Screens and moments that matter:** (the 3–6 things the viewer must see)
- **Numbers and claims:** (each with its meaning spelled out: "$50,000 in rewards", not "$50,000")
- **Voice:** yes / no · voice (af_heart default) · the script, one line per idea, 2–4 s each

## Look and sound
- **Brand:** colours, fonts (files), logo, key art
- **Style:** (a folder in styles/, or a new one)
- **References:** 1–3 videos, and what exactly to take from each (pace, transitions, type, colour)
- **Music:** synthesized (scripts/music.py) or a licensed song (file + start point). No uncleared tracks
